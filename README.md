## 🎯 Project Overview & Business Alignment
In corporate advisory settings like Financial Accounting Advisory Services (FAAS), digital finance transformations and transaction security are critical priorities for CFOs. This interactive learning asset was designed to train corporate professionals on identifying transactional anomalies, mitigating risk vectors, and shielding B2B digital payment ecosystems.

* **Target Audience:** Corporate Finance Professionals, Risk Analysts, and Enterprise Advisory Teams.
* **Core Learning Objectives:** Evaluate high-volume transaction risks, apply anomaly detection frameworks, and implement compliance guardrails.

---

## 🛠️ Instructional Design Framework & Methodologies

### 🧠 The ADDIE Translation
* **Analysis:** Evaluated complex corporate B2B payment data to isolate common fraud vectors and instructional gaps.
* **Design & Development:** Structured a micro-learning blueprint using **Articulate Rise 360** to maximize learner retention through interactive knowledge checks and branching logic.
* **Implementation & Evaluation:** Deployed globally via **GitHub Pages** to simulate an enterprise Learning Management System (LMS) deployment.

### 🤖 Generative AI as a Design Partner
Leveraging advanced prompt engineering frameworks, Generative AI tools were utilized to accelerate the instructional design lifecycle:
* **Persona Modeling:** Built precise target learner personas to rigorously pressure-test content engagement before live deployment.
* **Storyboard Prototyping:** Formulated dynamic scenario simulations and multi-step evaluation question banks.
